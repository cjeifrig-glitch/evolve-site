From the founder

Why I built this

I'm Charlie. I'm a senior in high school. A couple of years ago I scrolled for five hours in one sitting and I felt awful after. I downloaded a lot of screen time apps and I got around every one of them.

The first version of Evolve asked you to type why you wanted to open an app. It worked for a while, and then I caught myself lying to it. So I rebuilt it around the one thing I could not lie to, which is holding a button.

Evolve is free and it collects nothing. It is built for people who already want to change. I hope it helps you the way it helped me.

Charlie Eifrig

Questions

Fair questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Evolve is free to download and there is nothing to buy inside the app.

Can I get around it?

If you are determined to, yes. Evolve does not pretend to be unbeatable. It is here for the moments when you are not determined, which is most of them.

What is the difference between Easy and Hard?

Hard is the full price list above, with a 90 second cooldown after each session. Easy keeps every hold short and has no cooldown. Switching from Hard to Easy takes a ten second hold.

What does Evolve know about me?

Nothing leaves your phone. There is no account and no server. Your screen time is drawn by Apple on your device.

What do I need?

An iPhone on iOS 18 or later.
